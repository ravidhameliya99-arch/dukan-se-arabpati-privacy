Delete your game data

Dukan Se Arabpati · Developer: CodeCell · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Dukan Se Arabpati has no accounts of its own and no servers of its own. Your progress is kept on your phone and, if you sign in with Google Play Games, in your own Google account (Google Play Games "Saved Games"). Here is how to delete it.

1. Inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open Dukan Se Arabpati.
  2. Tap More → Account → Delete my saved progress.
  3. Tap Delete everything. This deletes your saved empire from your Google account and from your phone right away.

2. Without the game (in your Google account)

  1. Open play.google.com/games/profile and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Click Your data → Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Dukan Se Arabpati and click Delete.

The data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game (or in Android Settings → Apps → Dukan Se Arabpati → Storage → Clear storage).

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your game progress (stage, money, businesses, luxury items, badges, settings) and the names you typed in the game.
  • Kept by Google Play: records of things you bought (Google Play keeps purchase records; your purchases can be restored in the game with More → Shop → Restore purchases).
  • Data used by Google services (ads, statistics) is handled under Google's own policies, as explained in our privacy policy.
