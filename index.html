Privacy Policy

Dukan Se Arabpati (the "game") · Developer: CodeCell · Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to support.codecell@gmail.com.

1. Information kept on your phone

Your game progress (cash, stages, items, settings), the names you type in the game (your name and your shops' names) and a random player ID made by the game (for example K7QM-4XT2, used only so support can find your game) are saved on your phone. We do not ask for your real name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos or location. Please do not type private information into the name boxes. Android may include this game data in your phone's normal Google backup.

2. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game can save a copy of your progress to your Google account so you can continue on a new phone. To show who is signed in, the game reads your Play Games player name and profile picture (they are shown only inside the game and are not sent to us). The copy is stored by Google under your account and handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows only rewarded video ads, and only when you tap a "Watch ad" button. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location (from the IP address), device type and how you interact with ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time under More → Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying the No-Ads Pass gives every ad reward instantly without showing ads.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

All payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive a confirmation from Google Play of which item you bought, so the game can give it to you and restore it on a new phone.

5. Game statistics, crash and speed reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played, fix problems and keep it fast, the game may send information to Google Firebase services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage events such as the app was opened, the tutorial was finished, a stage was reached, a business was unlocked, an ad reward was collected (and which button), a purchase was made, and how long a session lasted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the game crashes, freezes or has an error, a report with the error details, the stage you were on, and device information.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: how long the game takes to start and how smoothly screens draw.

These include device information such as phone model, Android version, app version, country, and app-instance / installation identifiers. We do not use them to identify you personally, and we do not sell them.

6. Fair play and purchase checks

To keep the game fair, the app checks on your phone that purchases really come from Google Play, that the app is our genuine copy, and whether the phone is rooted (on modified copies and rooted phones, purchases and ad rewards are switched off). To stop changing the phone clock for extra rewards, the app reads the current time from Google's server (www.google.com); no personal information is sent with this request. These checks happen on your phone; their results are not sent to us, except as a count in the statistics above.

7. Help and support emails

If you tap More → Contact support, your email app opens with a message to us that already contains your player ID, the game and app version, your stage and your phone model. You decide whether to send it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

8. Reminders (notifications)

With your permission the game shows a few local reminders (for example when your manager's safe is full or your daily bonus is ready). They are created on your phone; no personal data is sent for them. You can turn them off in your phone's notification settings.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services named above, which work for the game, and when required by law. When you tap "Share", you choose the app (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Telegram), what to send and to whom. The share picture is made on your phone. "Save image" saves it only to your own phone's gallery.

All money in this game is virtual. It cannot be paid out or exchanged for real money.

10. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the game deletes the game data on your phone.
  • To delete your saved progress, in the game tap More → Account → Delete my saved progress. It deletes the copy in your Google account and on your phone. Other ways (without the game) are on our data deletion page.
  • Statistics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase for a limited time (statistics at most 14 months, crash reports 90 days) and then deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete them by writing to us with your player ID.
  • Data collected by Google services is kept and deleted according to Google's policies.

12. Security

The game talks to the internet only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and uses services from Google that protect data in transit. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
